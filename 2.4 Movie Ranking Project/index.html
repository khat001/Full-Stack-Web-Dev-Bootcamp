My top 3 movies of all time.

1. The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

First off all, the trilogy is a masterpiece. The production is timeless, it is a well-crafted media piece of all time.

2. Amadeus

Don't get me started with the "It's not an accurate movie!", almost all the historical movies are inaccurate, added with exaggeration and dramatization. The movie is a masterpiece, the acting is superb, the music is phenomenal, and the story is chef's kiss. The dramatic, one-sided rivalry between Mozart and Salieri is a great story to tell, and the movie did it justice.

3. The House that Jack Built

No, I'm, not a psychopath. This movie exceeds my expectations. The movie is different, unpredictable, and confusing. Well, he's an architect isn't he?
